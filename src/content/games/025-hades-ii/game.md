# Hades II

*Hades II* is on Nintendo Switch and Switch 2 for $29.99, and it launched on both on September 25, 2025. The Switch version runs at 60fps and 720p, the Switch 2 Edition runs at 120fps and 1080p in TV mode, and owners of the Switch version get the Switch 2 upgrade for free.

It is the sequel to [Supergiant's first Underworld roguelite, Hades](/games/hades/). You play Melinoë, Princess of the Underworld, and fight toward Chronos, the Titan of Time, with boons from more than a dozen Olympian gods, new weapons of Night, and witchcraft the first game did not have.

## Can you play Hades II on the original Switch?

Yes. The Switch version plays the full game at 60fps and 720p in both TV and handheld mode. A Switch 2 raises that to 120fps at 1080p in TV mode on a 120Hz display, or 60fps at 1080p in handheld mode, with sharper textures and effects.

## Which edition should you buy?

The digital Switch version and the digital Switch 2 Edition both cost $29.99. Buy the Switch version if you might play on both consoles, since the Switch 2 upgrade costs nothing. The $49.99 physical Switch 2 edition came out on November 20, 2025, also works on the original Switch, and adds an art booklet and a soundtrack download code. Saves carry across Switch, Switch 2, Steam, and the Epic Games Store.

## How does Hades II compare with Hades?

Start with *Hades* if you have played neither. It costs $24.99, its story is shorter, and it teaches the loop the sequel builds on. *Hades II* is the bigger game, with a new hero, two routes (down through the Underworld or up to the surface), and more systems to manage between runs. Reviews put a typical run at 25 to 45 minutes.
