# Moonlighter

*Moonlighter* is on Nintendo Switch for $24.99 and has been since November 5, 2018. You play Will, a shopkeeper in the village of Rynoka who dreams of becoming a hero, and each dive through the dungeon gates brings back loot to sell.

The shop is half the game. You set prices, watch how customers react, and spend the gold on village upgrades, new gear, and enchantments, so each dive starts stronger than the last. Like [Cult of the Lamb, it splits time between runs and a base](/games/cult-of-the-lamb/), with a heavier focus on trading. The *Between Dimensions* expansion is sold separately.

## Moonlighter or Moonlighter 2 on Switch?

Buy *Moonlighter* if you play on the original Switch, because the sequel is not available there. [Moonlighter 2: The Endless Vault launched on Switch 2](/games/moonlighter-2-the-endless-vault/) on September 2, 2026 for $29.99 and moves the shop to a new village, Tresna.
