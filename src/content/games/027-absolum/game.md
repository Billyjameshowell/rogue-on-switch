*Absolum* is on Nintendo Switch for $24.99. It launched on October 9, 2025, and a free Switch 2 Edition upgrade followed on September 17, 2026. Dotemu and Guard Crush Games, the studios behind *Streets of Rage 4*, made it with art and animation from Supamonks.

It is a side-scrolling fantasy brawler built around runs. Each run follows branching paths through the land of Talamh, with quests, characters, and bosses that change from one attempt to the next. Combat mixes combo chains with spells and counters. Where [Dead Cells is a solo side-scrolling roguelite](/games/dead-cells/), *Absolum* is a beat 'em up designed for two players.

## Does Absolum have co-op on Switch?

Yes. Two players can play on one Switch or online. When a second player joins, the world matches the less advanced player's progress, and each player keeps their own progress. The Switch 2 Edition adds local GameShare, runs at 60fps, and raises the resolution.

## How long is a run?

A full run usually takes 45 minutes to an hour, based on reviews and player reports. Between runs you earn items, quests, and permanent upgrades for your heroes.
