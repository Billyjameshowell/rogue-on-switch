# Caves of Qud

*Caves of Qud* is on Nintendo Switch for $29.99 and launched there on February 16, 2026. A free Switch 2 Edition upgrade has been announced, but it had no digital release date as of November 18, 2026.

It is a traditional turn-based roguelike set in a science fantasy world built on the ruins of ancient civilizations. You build a character from more than a hundred mutations and cybernetics, and every creature in the world is simulated with its own skills, gear, body parts, and loyalties to more than 70 factions. Walls can be dug through, melted, or eaten, so most problems have several solutions. For a permadeath dungeon crawl with friends, [Barony is the four-player first-person option](/games/barony/).

## Is Caves of Qud good on Switch?

Nintendo Life scored it 8 out of 10. The review praises how the controls map turn-based play to a controller, with ZR to pass turns, and notes that the interface feels cramped in handheld mode and that some loads cause brief slowdowns. The game supports the touch screen.

## Is Caves of Qud hard?

It can be. Classic mode uses permadeath, so a death ends the character. Roleplay mode lets you return to checkpoints at settlements, which makes the game easier to learn, though settlements are far apart.
