# Blue Prince

*Blue Prince* is on Nintendo Switch 2 for $29.99 and launched there on March 3, 2026. It is not on the original Switch. As the heir to Mt. Holly, you search the manor for its hidden Room 46 by choosing which room appears behind each door you open.

## Is Blue Prince a roguelike?

Partly. *Blue Prince* has roguelite structure: the floor plan resets every day, the rooms you can draft are random, and a few permanent upgrades to the estate survive the reset. It has no combat, enemies, or health, and most long-term progress is the knowledge you carry from one day to the next. We list it as a hybrid, so it suits puzzle fans and is a poor fit for anyone who wants roguelike combat.

Each day starts with 50 steps, and entering a room costs one. A day can last 15 minutes or more than an hour, depending on how much you stop to read and solve. For a run-based game where every turn is also a puzzle, [Into the Breach adds tactics and combat](/games/into-the-breach/).
