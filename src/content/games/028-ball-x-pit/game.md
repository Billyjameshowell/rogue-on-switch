# BALL x PIT

*BALL x PIT* is on Nintendo Switch for $14.99. It launched on October 15, 2025, and the Switch 2 Edition arrived as a free upgrade on October 28, 2025 with a higher resolution and frame rate.

The game crosses a brick-breaker with the level-up loop of [Vampire Survivors on Switch](/games/vampire-survivors/). Your heroes fire balls down into a pit of monsters, and as you level up you can fuse more than 60 kinds of ball into stronger combinations. Reviews put a typical run at 12 to 15 minutes, with longer runs near 20.

## What carries over between runs?

Resources from each run go into New Ballbylon, a town above the pit with more than 70 buildings to construct. Buildings add heroes and permanent bonuses, and heroes who stay home gather resources while you play.
