# BALL x PIT

*BALL x PIT* is on Nintendo Switch for $14.99. It launched on October 15, 2025, and the Switch 2 Edition arrived as a free upgrade on October 28, 2025 with a higher resolution and frame rate. We think it is great, easygoing fun: you fire balls at striking monsters and keep earning bigger, better balls.

The game crosses a brick-breaker with the level-up loop of [Vampire Survivors on Switch](/games/vampire-survivors/). Your heroes fire balls down into a pit, and as you level up you can fuse more than 60 kinds of ball into stronger combinations. Reviews put a typical run at 12 to 15 minutes, with longer runs near 20. The biomes and enemy designs are a highlight, and the soundtrack is one of the best we have heard this year.

## Is the town building in BALL x PIT worth it?

It grew on us. Resources from each run go into New Ballbylon, a town above the pit with more than 70 buildings to construct, and buildings add heroes and permanent bonuses. At first the town felt like a chore, and after a break it can look like a lot to manage again. Once we started optimizing the layout, though, it became the part we looked forward to, and it is the main way you get stronger.

## Is BALL x PIT too grindy?

It can feel that way. The difficulty curve leans on numbers going up, so some levels cannot be beaten until the town has raised your damage. Once one or two upgraded characters clear a level, the rest of the roster usually can too, so the walls come down quickly after that. We still think the curve could be smoother.
