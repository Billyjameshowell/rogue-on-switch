*PlateUp!* is on Nintendo Switch for $19.99 and launched there on February 15, 2024. It is a cooking roguelite for one to four players on one Switch or online, and it is one of our favorite co-op games to play as a couple. If you loved Overcooked, beat every level, and wanted more, this is the game to buy next.

Every run gives you a new restaurant with its own layout, recipes, and customers. Between shifts you buy appliances, rearrange the kitchen, and chain machines together until parts of it cook on their own. The in-run upgrades are a big part of the fun, and no two restaurants end up the same.

## Is PlateUp! like Overcooked?

The cooking will feel familiar, and it gets just as stressful when orders pile up. The difference is that *PlateUp!* never runs out of levels. Instead of a fixed set of stages, each run builds one restaurant over many days, and you design the kitchen yourself.

## What happens if a customer leaves in PlateUp!?

It ends the run. From day 4 on, a single customer who walks out without being served closes the restaurant for good, unless a card has given you an extra life. A restaurant you spent 30 minutes or more building can be gone in an instant, and in the first three days you can still restart the day instead.

## When can you franchise a restaurant in PlateUp!?

You can franchise once you complete day 15. You pick one of two franchise cards, and when the restaurant eventually closes you can save it as a franchise. A franchise keeps the restaurant's name, starting recipe, theme, franchise card, and three cards you choose, and you can start a new run with it as a harder follow-up. You can also keep playing past day 15 in overtime for as long as your restaurant holds out.

## Is PlateUp! good for co-op?

Yes. Up to four players can share one Switch or play online, and splitting the jobs between cooking, serving, and washing up is where the game shines. For a co-op pick with combat instead, try [Barony, a four-player dungeon crawler](/games/barony/) or [For The King on Switch](/games/for-the-king/).
