# Is Slay the Spire 2 on Switch?

No. *Slay the Spire 2* is not on Switch or Switch 2, and Mega Crit had not announced a Switch release date as of November 18, 2026. The sequel entered Steam Early Access on March 5, 2026 for $24.99 on Windows, macOS, and Linux, and Mega Crit says console and mobile versions likely won't arrive until Early Access is over.

Until then, [the original Slay the Spire is on Switch for $24.99](/games/slay-the-spire/) and remains the way to play the series on Nintendo hardware. Mega Crit confirms that the sequel runs on Steam Deck.

## What is new in Slay the Spire 2?

The sequel keeps the same climb of card battles, relics, and branching routes, and adds online co-op for up to four players on Steam. Mega Crit keeps adding content during Early Access, including new characters and game modes.

## What can you play on Switch while you wait?

Start with the first *Slay the Spire* if you have not finished it. For a newer deckbuilder, [Monster Train 2 came to Switch in May 2025](/games/monster-train-2/), and *Balatro* fits shorter sessions.
