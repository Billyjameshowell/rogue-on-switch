# UFO 50

*UFO 50* is one Nintendo Switch game with 50 games inside it. It costs $24.99 on the eShop and launched on Switch on August 7, 2025. We recommend it mainly for *Party House*, a deckbuilding roguelike in the collection that we rank among the best we have played lately.

Mossmouth, the team behind *Spelunky*, *Downwell*, and *Catacomb Kids*, made all 50 games in the style of a fictional 1980s studio called UFO Soft. The team stresses that these are full games with their own endings, not microgames, though some are short arcade games and others are long adventures. About half of them support local multiplayer.

## What is Party House in UFO 50?

*Party House* is game number 25 in the collection. You host a run of house parties and build a guest list the way you would build a deck of cards. Each party, guests arrive at random from your list. Guests earn popularity, which buys new guests, and cash, which makes the house bigger. Three troublemakers at once and the police shut the party down. You win by getting four star guests into one party within 25 parties.

## Is Party House as addictive as Balatro?

We think so. Like [Balatro, the poker roguelike on Switch](/games/balatro/), every run starts small and the fun comes from finding guests whose abilities stack into a huge turn. Each scenario changes which guests are for sale, so no two runs play out the same. A random scenario mode and a two-player versus mode add more variety.

## Which other UFO 50 games are roguelikes?

*Velgress* (game 7) is a vertical platformer where you climb out of a randomly generated pit while a spiked roller chases you from below, and a game over sends you back to the start. *Campanella 2* (game 35) is an action adventure through a space station where you pick your route through the regions and keep nothing between runs. Both reset fully when a run ends, and we have spent far less time with them than with *Party House*.

## Is UFO 50 worth buying for the roguelikes alone?

If you enjoy card roguelikes, *Party House* alone justifies the $24.99, and the other 49 games come along with it. Players who want a single long roguelite with permanent upgrades should look at [Hades II on Switch](/games/hades-ii/) instead.
