# Deadzone: Rogue

*Deadzone: Rogue* is on Nintendo Switch 2 for $24.99 and launched there on March 17, 2026. It is not on the original Switch.

You wake on a space station overrun by machines and fight through it sector by sector. A run has 30 sectors with a boss every 10, and you build your character from more than 30 weapons, elemental affixes, augments, and perks. Permanent upgrades between runs make each new attempt stronger. If you want a co-op shooter on the original Switch, [Risk of Rain 2 has third-person runs for up to four](/games/risk-of-rain-2/).

## Is Deadzone: Rogue good on Switch 2?

Reviews and player reports describe a well-equipped port with gyro aiming, mouse controls, and two performance modes. Consistent mode targets 30fps and Responsive mode targets about 40fps, and neither holds 60fps.

## Does Deadzone: Rogue have co-op?

Yes, online only. Up to three players can team up online, and play on a single console is solo. Difficulty is adjustable for each mission. Runs take about 25 to 45 minutes.
