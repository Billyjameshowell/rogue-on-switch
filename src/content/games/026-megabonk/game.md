# Is Megabonk on Switch?

No. *Megabonk* is not on Nintendo Switch or Switch 2, and its developer, vedinad, had not announced a console port as of November 18, 2026. It sells on Steam for $9.99 for Windows and Linux, and Valve rates it Steam Deck Verified.

*Megabonk* launched on September 18, 2025 as a 3D take on the loop that [Vampire Survivors made popular on Switch](/games/vampire-survivors/). Your weapons fire on their own while you move around a 3D map, and each level-up adds items that stack into builds strong enough to clear a crowd in seconds. Runs last 10 to 30 minutes depending on how many stages you play.

## What are the Megabonk games on the eShop?

The eShop lists games with similar names, such as *Megabonk Smash* from Evc Studio and *Gigabonk: Mega Survivors*. Other developers made them, and neither is a port of *Megabonk*. Check that the developer is vedinad before you buy anything that borrows the name.

## What should you play on Switch instead?

[BALL x PIT, a $14.99 brick-breaking roguelite](/games/ball-x-pit/), is the closest official match on Switch, with the same auto-fire power curve in runs of about 15 minutes. *Vampire Survivors* costs $4.99 on Switch and adds couch and online co-op for up to four players.
