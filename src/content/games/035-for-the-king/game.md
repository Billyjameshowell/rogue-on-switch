Yes, *For The King* is on Nintendo Switch. The $24.99 eShop version from IronOak Games and Curve Digital launched on May 9, 2019, and supports one to three players on one Switch or online. It blends tabletop board game movement with turn-based combat and roguelike risk.

A party of up to three heroes crosses a procedurally generated map of Fahrul, taking quests and fighting through random events. Dice-style rolls decide many outcomes, so luck matters as much as planning. If you prefer card-driven runs with less luck, [Slay the Spire is the classic deckbuilder on Switch](/games/slay-the-spire/), and [Darkest Dungeon covers similar party management](/games/darkest-dungeon/) with a darker tone.

## How does co-op work on Switch?

Two or three players can share one Switch, passing a single controller between turns, or play together online. The game is also fully playable solo, with one player controlling the whole party.

## How long is an adventure?

Most adventures take an hour or two, though longer campaigns and higher difficulties stretch that. There are three difficulty settings. Lore earned during each adventure, win or lose, buys new characters, items, and encounters in the Lore Store for future runs.

## How does it run on Switch?

Nintendo Life found the port well optimized when it reviewed it at launch, but called moving across the hex map with a stick clunky and noted there is no touch screen support. Player reports since then mention small text in handheld mode and occasional crashes, and the game autosaves often.

## Is For The King II on Switch?

No. *For The King II* launched on PC in 2023 and later came to Xbox and PlayStation. IronOak Games has said the original Switch did not meet its requirements, and no Switch or Switch 2 version has been announced in the US eShop. On Switch, the original is the one to buy.
