# Barony

*Barony* is on Nintendo Switch for $19.99 and launched there on August 1, 2023. It is a first-person dungeon crawler with permadeath, written as a tribute to *Ultima Underworld* and *Daggerfall*, and one to four players can play together.

You descend through the dungeon beneath the town of Hamlet to destroy Baron Herx, a lich. There are 13 classes, and each one improves at the skills you practice during a run. Few games on the site play like it, so the nearest match is [Caves of Qud, another traditional permadeath roguelike](/games/caves-of-qud/).

## Is Barony good for co-op on Switch?

Yes. Up to four players can share one Switch in split-screen, connect over local wireless, or play online, including with players on PC through crossplay.

## How long is a run?

A full clear of all 35 floors takes an experienced player about three to five hours. Almost nothing carries over after a death, so your progress comes from learning the classes, monsters, and traps.
