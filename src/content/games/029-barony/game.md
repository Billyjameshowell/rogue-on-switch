# Barony

*Barony* is on Nintendo Switch for $19.99 and launched there on August 1, 2023. It is a first-person permadeath dungeon crawler for one to four players, and in co-op it is one of the most chaotic and funny games we have played on the console.

Turning Wheel first released it on PC in 2015 and has kept updating it for a decade, which is why it has such a devoted community. You descend through the dungeon beneath the town of Hamlet to destroy the lich Baron Herx, choosing from 13 classes that improve at the skills you practice. The nearest match on the site is [Caves of Qud, another traditional permadeath roguelike](/games/caves-of-qud/), though Barony is far more of a group game.

## What is Barony like in co-op?

It is brutal and hilarious. On our first co-op run, our partner found an amulet and handed it to us. We put it on, it turned out to be a cursed Amulet of Strangulation, and it choked our character to death on the spot. Later we opened a door and a giant pumpkin rolled out and flattened us. Moments like these are why Barony is so much fun with friends, and why it is not a relaxing game.

## Is Barony hard to learn?

Yes. The game explains very little at the start, and that can be frustrating for the first few runs. It gets rewarding once you learn which items are safe to equip, how traps warn you, and how each class plays. Almost nothing carries over after a death, so that knowledge is your real progress.

## Is Barony good for co-op on Switch?

Yes. Up to four players can share one Switch in split-screen, connect over local wireless, or play online, including with players on PC through crossplay.

## How long is a run?

A full clear of all 35 floors takes an experienced player about three to five hours.
